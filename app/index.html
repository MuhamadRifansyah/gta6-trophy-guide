⚠ ONE POSSIBLE MISSABLE — Positive Mental Attitude

Meditate at every spot across Leonida. The first meditation, at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, can only be done then: after the mission “Down to Earth”, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. PowerPyx is unsure whether that first meditation is required for the trophy, so do it in Chapter 1 to be safe. Everything else can be done after the story.

Roadmap to the Platinum

Three steps, about 200 hours. The story is 5 chapters and takes 60–80 hours; after it you keep playing in free-roam, and everything needed for 100% is still reachable. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots + 1 autosave).

Step 1 — Finish the story, do side content as it appears 60–80 hours

Play the story and enjoy it. The 100% checklist is not viewable until after the story, but progress you make during it already counts. Do optional missions as they appear (there are 200–300+, only 100 are needed for 100%). Scan vehicles with the WAiNK app (hold Up, then R1) and study animals with binoculars (hold L1 for the weapon wheel, zoom with Up, hold R1 to study). Do the Chapter 1 meditation — see the warning above.

Step 2 — 100% Completion checklist & 70 of 80 challenges about 100 hours

After the story, the checklist opens under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is lenient — closer to 60% true completion. Partial requirements only: scan 200 of 277 vehicles, study 100 of 255 animals, finish 70 of 80 challenges, 15 of 18 meditations and 15 of 18 card sets. Only robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests are required in full. Nothing on the list is missable.

Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and clean-up about 10 hours

Strangers & Freaks are not needed for 100%, so finish the 8 strands here, plus any leftover miscellaneous trophies. Once A Long Road (100%) pops, Vice Got a Price! follows immediately.
