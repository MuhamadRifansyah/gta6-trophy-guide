Leonida Trophy Guide is an offline tracker and guide for all 55 Grand Theft Auto VI trophies on PlayStation 5 — 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze. Click a trophy to mark it earned; progress saves in your browser. Filter by story, side quests, collectibles, activities, robberies, or the one possible missable. A spoiler setting (on by default) hides the 28 hidden trophies until you earn them or turn spoilers off. Every trophy carries a guide written in our own words with a named source, and the Roadmap tab lays out the three-step path to the Platinum in about 200 hours, with the 100% completion thresholds and the Chapter 1 meditation warning. No network needed: the whole guide runs from its own files.

Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Build the best GTA 6 trophy guide" bounty, $250 in USDC. Play GTA 6? Try make.fun: https://make.fun/r/1fc9hzy4

## Run it

Node 22 or later, nothing to install. Serve the app and open it:

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Or build the dossier the judge reads:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Then open `out/app/index.html`. Search, filter, toggle spoilers, export and import your progress.
